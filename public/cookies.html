Cookie Policy

Last updated: September 2026

This notice explains the cookies and similar technologies used on Pondco Online, why they are used, and how you can control them.

What cookies are

Cookies are small text files stored on your device by your browser. They can remember preferences, keep a session working, measure usage, or — in the case of advertising cookies — help show ads relevant to your interests. Similar technologies, such as local storage and tracking pixels, work in comparable ways.

Cookies we use

Strictly necessary cookies. Essential hosting and security services may use strictly necessary technical mechanisms to provide and protect the site — for example, load balancing, security filtering, and remembering a consent choice. These are required for the site to function and cannot be disabled through site settings.

Advertising cookies. We use third-party advertising services, including Google AdSense, which may place cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this site and other sites on the Internet. Google's advertising cookies enable Google and its partners to select and measure ads. These cookies are set by the advertising providers, not by Pondco Online, and are governed by the providers' own policies.

Analytics. If we enable web analytics in the future, we will update this notice to describe what is measured and offer an appropriate choice.

Your choices

You have several ways to control cookies:

  • Browser settings. All major browsers let you block or delete cookies, either entirely or per site. Blocking strictly necessary cookies may impair site functionality; blocking advertising cookies will generally result in less relevant ads rather than no ads.
  • Google Ads Settings. You can opt out of Google's personalized advertising at Google Ads Settings.
  • Industry opt-outs. You can opt out of interest-based advertising from participating companies at www.aboutads.info.

Changes to this notice

If our use of cookies changes — for example, if we add analytics or new advertising providers — we will update this notice and the "Last updated" date above, and provide an appropriate consent choice where required by law.
