About PondCo

PondCo is an independent pond-owner resource. We publish plain-language, practical guides for people who build, keep, or are thinking about a backyard pond or water garden — the planning, the digging, the gear, the seasonal work, and the problems everyone eventually meets.

What we publish

Complete guides that show their working: the volume and pump-sizing math with real numbers, the temperature thresholds for feeding fish, the ranking of algae fixes by whether they address the cause. When a figure is a rule of thumb rather than a measured fact, we label it as one. When practice varies by climate, we say so.

What we are not

PondCo is not a pond contractor, a retailer, or a substitute for professional advice on your specific water feature. We do not sell ponds or equipment ourselves. If a guide touches on electrical work, local codes, or anything structural, the right move is a qualified local professional — our material helps you talk to them, not replace them.

How we handle products and money

PondCo is reader-supported. The site carries advertising (served by Google AdSense). Product links, when published, are Amazon Associates links, clearly marked as advertising, which can earn a commission at no extra cost to you. Editorial decisions — what the criteria are, what a guide recommends — are made independently of advertising and affiliate relationships. We do not publish fake reviews, invented ratings, or endorsements we cannot stand behind.

Corrections

We correct errors when we find them. If you spot something wrong or outdated, email us and we will investigate and fix it.
